# Detecting Pre-Symptomatic APOE ε4 Alzheimer's Risk from EEG Connectivity

## Summary

MSc Applied Artificial Intelligence dissertation project. A graph neural network (GNN) applied to EEG functional connectivity to detect pre-symptomatic APOE ε4 genetic risk in cognitively healthy middle-aged adults, compared to and fused with a tabular biomarker baseline.

## Data

Dzianok, P. and Kublik, E. (2024) ‘PEARL-Neuro database: EEG, fMRI, health and lifestyle data of middle-aged people at risk of dementia’, Scientific Data, 11(1), p. 276
