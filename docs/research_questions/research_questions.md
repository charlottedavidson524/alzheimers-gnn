Primary question:

- Can an interpretable GNN on EEG-derived brain connectivity graphs distinguish APOE e4 risk carriers from non-carriers in healthy middle-aged adults?

Secondary questions:

- Do multimodal biomarkers (blood, psychometrics, genetic risk factors beyond APOE) carry independent predictive information beyond what EEG captures?

- Does explicit multimodal fusion (late or intermediate) improve classification over either modality alone?

- Which brain regions and network patterns does an interpretable GNN identify as discriminative, and are these consistent with prior AD literature?

Motivation:

- In people this young (middle aged), they are rarely showing symptoms yet. Idea being, if you can classify the likelihood of someone having genes tied to Alzhiemers, and do this in a cheap and effective manor, you can somewhat quantify their risk of developing the disease and they can focus on prevention.
